The Quiet Stable

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This notice explains how The Quiet Stable (the "game") handles your information. The game is made by Almontaser Bellah Ahmed ("I", "me"). It applies to every version of the game, including iPhone, iPad, Mac and PC.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (your horse's name, care history, tricks learned and settings) in a save file on your own device. That file never leaves your device. I cannot see it, and nobody else receives it. Deleting the game deletes the save file.

The game checks your device's clock so your horse can notice how long you've been away. That time is only used inside the game on your device.

What the game does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or send data to me or anyone else.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It has no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

Apple, Steam and other stores

If you download the game from the App Store, TestFlight or Steam, that store handles your purchase and account under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share crash reports or beta feedback through TestFlight, Apple passes that information to me so I can fix problems, and I only use it for that. You control this in your device settings.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently, I will update this notice before that version is released and change the date at the top.
