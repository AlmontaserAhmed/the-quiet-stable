Support

Something not working, or got an idea? Email almonicstudios@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

It helps if you include your device (for example "iPhone 15" or "iPad Air"), the game version (shown in Settings) and what happened just before the problem.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There's no cloud save yet, so deleting the game deletes your progress.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline.

Is there anything to buy inside the game?

No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

I'm a beta tester. How do I send feedback?

In TestFlight, take a screenshot while playing and tap "Share Beta Feedback", or open TestFlight, choose The Quiet Stable and tap "Send Beta Feedback". Email works too.
